AI mode

Offline mode is always free and unlimited. AI mode gives more natural rewrites using Google Gemini with your own free key. The key stays on this phone.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in.
  2. Tap “Create API key”, copy it, and paste it below.

With AI mode on, your text is sent to Google to be rewritten. Google sets the free daily limit for your key; when it is reached, ParaLine finishes the rest offline.
