ParaLine Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Summary

ParaLine has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. The developer does not collect, store or receive any of your data.

Offline mode

In offline mode all text is processed on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. The app stores your draft and your settings on your device only.

AI mode (optional)

AI mode is off until you turn it on by adding your own Google Gemini API key. When AI mode is on, the text you choose to rephrase is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) over an encrypted connection, together with your API key, so that Google can return the rewritten text. Google handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API. The developer of ParaLine never receives your text or your key.

Your API key is stored only on your device, is excluded from device backups, and can be deleted at any time in the app settings ("Remove key") or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app requests only the Internet permission, which is used solely for AI mode.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
